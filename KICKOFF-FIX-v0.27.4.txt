Football Legacy v0.27.4 — Actual post-goal restart fix

Root cause repaired
- The goal replay left the internal `celebrate` flag set to 1.
- The main match loop therefore re-entered the celebration branch forever after the replay and never allowed the kick-off pass to continue.
- The flag and scorer state are now cleared before the conceding team is placed at the centre spot.

Quick Play / interactive single-player match
- The replay ends.
- The team that conceded is placed at the centre spot.
- Its centre pass is executed immediately.
- The normal match loop then continues.

Career detailed match viewer
- The viewer now finds the next kick-off by the team that conceded, searching the full remaining frame list rather than only a narrow window.
- Playback resumes from that centre pass immediately after the goal sequence.

No other gameplay, database, menu, creation, physics, ratings, officiating or Career systems were changed.
