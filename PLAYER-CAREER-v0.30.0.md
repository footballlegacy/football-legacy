# Football Legacy Player Career — v0.30.0 Test Copy

This is a separate full-game test copy. It does not replace the Career Integrity copy or Josh's match work.

## Open it

Open `index.html`, then choose **Create Legacy**, **Grassroots to Glory**, or **Player Career**. The original Grassroots mode remains intact and has its own direct start-menu button.

## Included now

- Start at age 14 through an existing club academy or local grassroots football.
- Bidirectional career ladder: grassroots, district football, academy trial, U14, U16, U18, reserves, rare first-team cameo, first team, veteran football, and a possible return to grassroots.
- Releases are real career events rather than game over. Strong local form can rebuild scouting attention and create a redemption route.
- Every match is permanently recorded with date, opponent, competition, result, minutes, rating, goals, assists, captaincy and key-moment status.
- Club appearances remain separate from youth and senior international appearances, so stint statistics are not inflated by international matches.
- Development, real age-based decline, fitness, fatigue, morale, training, objectives and coach trust.
- No forced career-length cap. Retirement is the player's choice.
- Squad Hub with competition for the shirt, individual teammate relationships, manager trust and fan connection.
- Agent screen with contracts and transfer interest.
- Manager conversations about minutes, role, feedback and pressure.
- Outside Football choices covering family, rest, extra work, social media and nightlife trade-offs.
- Youth and senior international selection, separate youth/senior records, national-manager relationships and earnable international captaincy.
- Post-retirement handoff into the existing manager-career identity with the playing record attached.
- Separate Player Career save and Continue Player Career card on the main menu.

## Deliberately not included yet

- Football politics career.
- Referee career.
- Full 3D handoff for key Player Career matches. Matches use the statistical career simulation for this version while Josh's match engine remains separate.
- Deep international tournaments, qualifying groups and squad-selection screens. International call-ups, appearances and captaincy are working, but tournament structure is a later layer.
- Full synchronized transfer world with every manager-career club. The academy picker uses the real game club database; later senior offers currently use a small fictional pool.
- Injuries, boot deals, awards, interviews and full story scenes. The data structure and screens are ready to be expanded, but these have not been pretended complete.
