Football Legacy Integration Pass 3 — Officiating Realism

Quick Play match engine
- Added contextual dive input: X on keyboard, D-pad down on controller.
- Added contextual shirt-pull input: E on keyboard, RB/R1 on controller.
- Added contact, ball-first, near-miss, repeated-pull and referee-view measurements.
- Added era-based challenge tolerance, referee strictness/control, match temperature, dissent, handbags, rare fights and persistent player feuds.
- Added VAR checks only for match years 2020 and later.
- Added a post-goal sequence watchdog and a forced AI kick-off fallback so the conceding opposition restarts after a goal.

Career Mode detailed match simulation
- Added era-based physicality and officiating thresholds.
- Added simulation, shirt-pull, hard-challenge, dissent, confrontation and rare fight incidents.
- Added persistent player feud memory to Career saves.
- Added match-temperature history to detailed match records.
- VAR is hard-gated to 2020 onward and the timeline activation has been moved into 2020.

Boundary
- Career still uses its existing simulation engine; it is not connected to the interactive Quick Play match engine.
- No menu, creation-tool, club database, player-face, kit, transfer, history or match-physics redesign was made in this pass.
