Football Legacy — Match Realism v0.28

Changes in this build:
- Corrected both goal nets so they extend behind the goal line rather than into the pitch.
- Made held passes substantially stronger; a full bar can now hit proper long diagonals and cross-field switches.
- Reduced full pass-charge time from 920 ms to 760 ms.
- Added visibly different jogging and sprinting speeds, body lean, stride length and arm movement.
- Sprinting now costs more stamina; jogging is slower and more economical.
- Kept set-piece setup delays, but removed the visible set-piece countdown.
- Added a low-driven shot: double-tap S while in possession.
- Normal quick-tap S remains a standard shot; hold S to charge a stronger normal shot.
- Added cleaner contact shadows, a brighter selected-player ring, a slightly improved ball and finer goal-net mesh.

Controls:
Arrows — jog
Shift — sprint
A — pass (hold for distance)
S — shoot
S twice — low-driven shot
D — tackle
Q + direction — skill
Q twice — roulette
W — switch player
Esc — pause
