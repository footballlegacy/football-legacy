Football Legacy Match Graphics v0.39

Face rebuild, based directly on the latest v0.38 build:
- Removed every separate 3D brow, eye, cheek, nose and mouth block from the face
- Replaced the head with one softened, bevelled 2.5D head mesh
- Added one flush face layer containing the eyes, brows, nose shading, nostrils and a clearly drawn mouth
- Kept hair as a shallow cap rather than large blocks around the face
- No gameplay or set-piece changes

The aim of this pass is specifically to stop facial parts sticking out of the head while making the mouth and eyes readable.
