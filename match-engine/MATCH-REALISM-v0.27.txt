Football Legacy Match Realism v0.27

Focused changes to match rules and dead-ball presentation:
- Fouls now identify the offender and victim in referee decisions.
- Foul severity distinguishes ordinary, tactical, reckless and dangerous challenges.
- Yellow cards are no longer automatically converted into a red on the next ordinary foul.
- Second-yellow dismissals and straight red cards are handled separately.
- Red cards remove the player from active play.
- Referee decision cut-scenes pause live play and the match clock.
- Penalty decisions receive a dedicated referee presentation.
- Set-piece delays now vary realistically by restart type.
- Corners, penalties and free kicks take longer to organise than throw-ins.
- Card/foul cut-scenes must finish before the restart can be taken.
- Existing match engine, graphics, controls and instant-result files are preserved.
