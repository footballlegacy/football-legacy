Football Legacy Match Realism v0.57 — Football Intelligence AI Overhaul

This build keeps the working match engine and adds a new decision/positioning layer around it.

Implemented:
- Scan → plan → execute loop for AI ball carriers.
- Pre-reception scanning: intended receivers prepare a likely next action before controlling the ball.
- Separate decision quality from technical execution.
- Contextual team phases: settle, probe, accelerate, recycle, protect, block, trap and press.
- Match-state risk: scoreline, match minute, role, tactical mentality, fatigue and team phase affect choices.
- Era profiles affect directness, dribbling, recycling, compactness, crossing and pressing coordination.
- More varied off-ball movement: support, check short, stay wide, overlap, underlap, decoy, back-post and hold runs.
- Improved team shape: lines shift together and defensive compactness changes with phase/tactics.
- Stable press assignments instead of constantly changing chasers.
- Pressing behaviour now delays, shows attackers outside, uses cover support and creates touchline traps.
- Centre-backs are less likely to abandon the defensive line without cover.
- Expanded pass evaluation: progression, receiver pressure, body shape, passing lane, switch potential, offside risk, distance, risk tolerance and recycling value.
- Passing choices are selected naturally from several good options rather than always choosing a perfect maximum.
- Safer backwards/recycling passes are used when pressure or match state requires them.
- Contextual clearances from defenders in dangerous situations.
- Shooting uses an expected-goal-style decision check including distance, angle, body shape, pressure, blockers and teammate chances.
- AI passes rather than shoots when a teammate has a clearly better chance.
- Crossing only happens when useful targets are in the box and the lane is viable.
- Dribbling is reserved for players with space, control and a football reason to carry.
- AI shot trajectories now vary between low, driven, rising and curled attempts.
- First-touch success now accounts for incoming pace, body orientation, pressure, fatigue, control, weather and whether the player scanned beforehand.
- Contextual heavy touches replace purely arbitrary errors.
- Fatigue affects scanning, decisions, movement, pressing and execution context.

Preserved unchanged:
- SculptGL face/head mesh
- Hair and player graphics
- Celebrations and crowd-jump sequences
- User controls
- Camera modes
- Kits and stadium
- Set-piece system
- Fouls/cards
- Minimap and sound
