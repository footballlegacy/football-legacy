Football Legacy Match Graphics v0.62 — Player Body Refinement

BODY-ONLY CHANGES
- Replaced the rectangular torso and pelvis silhouette with shared low-poly rounded/tapered geometry.
- Improved athletic proportions through the shoulders, waist, hips, thighs, calves, arms and forearms.
- Rounded the shoulder, sleeve, elbow, knee, ankle and limb transitions while preserving the stylised art direction.
- Reshaped the shirt and shorts so the kit follows the body rather than reading as flat stacked panels.
- Rebuilt boots with a rounded upper, shaped toe, instep, heel, sole, tongue and laces.
- Refined hands with a shaped palm and thumb while keeping mesh cost modest.
- Improved running posture with restrained forward lean, torso/hip counter-rotation, natural opposite arm swing and more believable knee flex.
- Added stance-foot compensation and ankle rotation so planted boots remain steadier against the pitch during locomotion.
- Added a quieter, slightly asymmetric idle stance instead of rigid vertical posing.
- Reused shared body geometries across all players to limit memory and geometry cost.

UNCHANGED
- Head, sculpted face geometry, facial materials, hair and neck code.
- Gameplay, AI, ball physics, camera, stadium, pitch, crowd, lighting, sound, UI, controls, celebrations and set pieces.
