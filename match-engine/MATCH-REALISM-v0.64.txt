Football Legacy Match Realism v0.64

P — EVERY HUMAN CELEBRATION

After the user's team scores, press P to launch the special three-scene celebration:

1. Every active humanoid in the ground is sent toward the goalscorer: both teams, both goalkeepers, referee, assistant referees, managers and all individually modelled supporters. Static crowd point layers are hidden during the sequence so no crowd remains frozen in the stands.
2. Hard cut outside the stadium. Spectators run through entrances while the camera slowly pulls out, creating a human pile that rises above the stadium roof.
3. Immediate hard cut to a stylised rotating Earth. A new human pile visibly grows from one point on the globe.

The sequence ends by restoring the stadium and crowd, then returning to the normal kickoff.
P remains the normal pause key at every other time.

No normal celebration, player model, gameplay, AI, ball physics, camera, stadium, pitch, lighting, crowd setup, sound, UI, controls or set-piece mechanics were intentionally changed.
