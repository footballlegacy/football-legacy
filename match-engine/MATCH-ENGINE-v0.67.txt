Football Legacy Match Engine v0.67
Player Bodies and Goal-Kick Presentation

PLAYER BODY VISUAL PASS
- Five performance-safe footballer archetypes: average athletic, powerful, lean, short/stocky and tall/slender.
- More athletic silhouettes with sloped shoulders, shaped torsos, narrower waists, fuller hips and tapered limbs.
- Low-poly kit shaping for sleeves, shirt hem, shorts legs, sock cuffs and boot transitions.
- More recognisable hands and football boots using shared geometry.
- Separate skin, fabric, shorts, sock and boot materials with restrained roughness variation.
- Running and idle posture refined with acceleration lean, turn lean, hip rotation, elbow bend, stride variation and deceleration response.
- Improved local foot planting to reduce visible floating and sliding.
- The imported sculpted head/face construction is unchanged.

BALL AND GOAL KICKS
- Ball is slightly smaller visually while its gameplay collision radius remains unchanged.
- Goal kicks use a legal goal-area position and wait for opponents to leave the penalty area.
- Goal-kick and kickoff cameras remain at the normal wide broadcast view.
- AI goalkeepers kick long most of the time, with tactical and era variation.
- On user goal kicks: A plays short; S drives a long kick upfield.
- Added a stronger goalkeeper goal-kick body action.

PRESERVED
- Gameplay AI outside goal-kick behaviour, ball physics, controls, set pieces, stadium, pitch, crowd, lighting, sound, UI, celebrations and hidden testing keys remain as in v0.66.
