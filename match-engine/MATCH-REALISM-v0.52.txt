Football Legacy Match Realism v0.52

Goal celebration choices

After a Ribble End goal, press a number within roughly three seconds:

1 — Run to the nearest corner flag and celebrate with the team.
2 — Run to the touchline crowd and get mobbed by supporters and teammates.
3 — Perform a backflip while teammates gather around.
4 — Retrieve the ball from the net and carry it back to the centre spot.
5 — Sprint away with both hands raised while teammates chase behind.

If no number is pressed, celebration 1 is selected automatically.
Opposition goals continue with an automatic corner celebration.

The existing face, hair, player models, kits, gameplay, camera, controls, minimap and sound are unchanged apart from these goal-celebration behaviours and the celebration-choice overlay.
