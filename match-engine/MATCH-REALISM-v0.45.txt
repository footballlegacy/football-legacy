Football Legacy Match Realism v0.45

Focused fixes:
- Kickoffs now begin with a compulsory pass from the taker to a second player positioned beside the centre spot.
- The shoot button cannot be used to start a kickoff; the game prompts the user to pass.
- AI kickoffs also use the designated second player.
- Goal celebrations now assign fixed corner targets and explicitly use the sprint gait.
- The scorer runs to the corner rather than being moved there with a sliding-looking motion.
- Teammates follow in a staggered group, then switch to the existing cheering animation on arrival.

No other gameplay or graphics systems were intentionally changed.
