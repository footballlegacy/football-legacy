Football Legacy Match Graphics v0.35

Further player-model softening:
- Rounded the head silhouette away from square blocks using multi-sided prism/cylinder shapes
- Softened cheeks, jaw, forehead, hair and facial-feature blocks
- Arms and legs changed from sharp boxes to smoother octagonal limb shapes
- Hands and boots retained but sit on the smoother limbs
- Running cycle toned down further to avoid the fast "Scooby-Doo" leg whirl
- Materials smoothed slightly so edges read less jagged while keeping the 2.5D look

Gameplay and set-piece systems unchanged.
