FIFA-style skill gestures · restored

Step-overRS ↑ ↻ →  or  RS ↑ ↺ ←Start the right stick up, rotate a quarter-circle to either side, then release. The leading leg lifts and sweeps over the ball before the exit touch.
RouletteRS ↓ ↻ ←  or  RS ↓ ↺ →Start the right stick down, rotate a half-circle around either side, then release. The player shields the ball through a full body rotation.
