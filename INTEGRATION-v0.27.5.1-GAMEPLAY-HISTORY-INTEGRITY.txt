FOOTBALL LEGACY v0.27.5.1 — GAMEPLAY & HISTORY INTEGRITY

SCOPE
This pass changes only the items requested after v0.27.4: Quick Play/Career officiating, modern Quick Play counterpart profiles, historical promotion/relegation placement, historical player mobility and career records, player-honours presentation, Quick Play team-management handoff, and the inbox family-link routing fault. The working v0.27.4 post-goal kick-off functions are deliberately unchanged.

PROMOTION / RELEGATION HISTORY
- Historical fast-forward tables and recorded league movements are now authoritative.
- The old historical-start snapshot override is disabled; it can no longer force a club back to a database tier after the simulation has placed it elsewhere.
- On load/start, the selected club's current tier is reconciled from the latest archived tier plus the exact recorded movement row.
- A third-place First Division finish with no recorded movement remains First Division and is not counted as either promotion or relegation.
- The 1957–58 regional reorganisation has a separate safeguard rather than being treated as ordinary promotion/relegation.

HISTORICAL PLAYER MOBILITY
- Movement rates vary by era rather than using one modern transfer rate throughout football history.
- Strong club control and locality matter most before 1963; movement rises after the 1963 reforms, rises again after the late 1970s, and is substantially freer in the post-Bosman period.
- Age, contract status, appearances, squad role, loyalty, ambition, ability/tier fit, positional need, geography, tenure and previous club count influence moves.
- Transfers close the old club spell, open a new club spell, add a transfer-history row and preserve the full club history when the player retires.
- Unattached players continue ageing and cannot reappear decades later as active signings.

QUICK PLAY PLAYER PROFILES
- 92 fictional Football Legacy clubs across four current English professional divisions.
- 2,116 fictional players, 23 per club.
- Every player has one unique counterpart identity for position, age, role, overall and attribute distribution; no counterpart is duplicated within two fictional players.
- Fictional display names, faces, club names, badges and kits remain unchanged.
- 2,068 profiles use player-level FC 26 records. 48 lower-league/youth profiles use individual current-squad-derived profiles where a complete public rating record was unavailable.
- The core rating snapshot is dated 19 September 2025, with targeted 2026/27 roster corrections dated 6 August 2026. This is a fixed calibration dataset, not a live transfer feed.

QUICK PLAY TEAM MANAGEMENT
- Formation, starting XI and seven-player bench.
- Existing engine-supported instructions exposed: mentality, width, support runs, pressing, tempo, defensive line and passing directness.
- The selected lineup, bench, tactics, kit and squad are all passed to the existing Quick Play match engine.

DIVING / SHIRT PULLING / OFFICIATING
- Contexts represented: baseless, reasonable, reasonable+, reasonable++, reasonable+++.
- Marginal reasonable+ contact produces a defender foul exactly 10% of the time.
- Inside that called-foul branch, defender dissent produces a yellow exactly 20% of the time.
- Three consecutive shirt pulls are recorded as a distinct persistent-pulling incident.
- A called three-pull incident carries the requested 30% yellow-card chance.
- Interactive action states include pull, stumble, dive, appeal and recovery.
- Rare forceful referee-card gesture included.
- Referee perception, reputation, match temperature and era tolerance affect decisions.

POST-CARD CONFRONTATION
- During the five-second prompt after a yellow or red card, press F to trigger a confrontation.
- Three visible outcomes: small push and breakup; mass gathering; full brawl.
- Full brawls can very rarely bring supporters onto the pitch before stewards intervene.
- The carded player remains an anchor participant after a red card rather than disappearing before the confrontation begins.
- The same three-level incident logic is represented in Career detailed-match records.

VAR
- VAR is unavailable before 2020 and active from 2020 onward.
- Silent checks, earpiece behaviour, replay-angle count, obstruction/evidence quality, monitor review, final decision and inconclusive evidence are represented.
- Inconclusive evidence can leave the original decision in place.

PERSISTENT REPUTATION
- Diver, hard-man, dissent, supporter-hostility and targeting values persist.
- Feuds and reputations influence later suspicion, confrontation likelihood, targeting and atmosphere.

PLAYER HONOURS
- The player page now uses the same cabinet hierarchy as the club trophy page: HONOUR / WON / WINNING SEASONS.
- Team trophies and individual awards are separated.
- Repeated wins are grouped by competition with a total count and all recorded winning seasons.

INBOX ROUTING
- Family buttons now require whole family-related words. A footballer's surname such as Jackson no longer matches the letters “son” and therefore no longer produces an OPEN FAMILY button in a match report.
