# Football Legacy v0.28.6 — Playtest Notes

## New in this build

- Controller navigation across all playable menus and overlays.
- D-pad or left stick moves the orange focus highlight; Cross selects; Circle goes back.
- Match controls remain isolated from menu controls during live play.
- DualSense right-stick stepover and roulette recognition is restored and more forgiving.
- Three long-form R3 dive performances cycle in this order: subtle, flop, ridiculous roll.
- Every dive has a replay whose duration matches the performance.
- Goal, red-card, foul and dive shots now favour held angles and deliberate cuts over constant camera movement.
- Goal celebrations use longer camera holds while the actual animation continues to play freely.

## Ten-minute test

1. Launch single player with `START-PS5-SINGLE-PLAYER.command`.
2. Press Options and navigate the pause menu using only the controller.
3. Open Controller & reaction preview, then return with Circle.
4. In possession, perform RS up-to-side for a stepover and RS down half-circle for a roulette.
5. Press R3 on three separate eligible possessions to see Subtle, Flop and Ridiculous Roll.
6. Trigger a goal and watch for stable close shots plus fewer, slower camera changes.
7. Connect a second controller and launch the two-player command.

## Known limit

Text fields in creation screens can be focused with the controller, but typing still uses the keyboard.
